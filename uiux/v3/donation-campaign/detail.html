About campaign

Download proposal

“Build a School for Điện Biên” aims to replace temporary bamboo shelters with three resilient classrooms, a reading corner, and a safe play zone. Once completed, 180 pupils will study in a weather-proof environment and have access to curated books for the first time.

The total budget is 20,000 MMN (≈ 1B VND). 60% covers construction materials and labor, 20% equips classrooms with desks, chairs, and solar lighting, 10% funds the library, and 10% supports teacher training and student life-skills workshops.

Key milestones:

  • Oct 2025 · Complete legal paperwork, select local contractor.
  • Nov–Dec 2025 · Build classrooms and playground foundations.
  • Jan 2026 · Install library shelves, books, and electrical systems.
  • Feb 2026 · Handover ceremony and on-site life-skills festival.

Monthly transparency reports will be shared with donors, including photos, expense breakdowns, and blockchain transaction links. Mezon CSR performs internal audits to ensure compliance and maximize impact.

Impact snapshot

180 students will learn in safe classrooms, three additional teachers will be onboarded, and a community mini-library will open by February 2026.
